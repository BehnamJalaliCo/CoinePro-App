6.1) Pro Chart's relationship with the exchanges

Pro Chart works officially with the LBank and Ourbit exchanges. That is why membership goes through Pro Chart's dedicated link: your account in the exchange's system is tied to Pro Chart, and that tie is what makes automatic verification and the service possible.

Two things should be clear:

  • There is no extra cost to you. The fee you pay at the exchange is that exchange's ordinary fee and does not change because of your Pro Chart membership.

6.2) Membership conditions

To access signals and copy trading:

  1. Register at LBank or Ourbit through Pro Chart's dedicated link. This step is not optional — an account created through any other link is not tied to Pro Chart in the exchange's system and cannot be verified.
  2. Bring the account balance to at least 50 USDT.
  3. Enter your exchange account's UID in the app.
  4. Verification runs automatically through the exchange's own API. Once confirmed, access is enabled.

If you already had an account opened without the dedicated link, let support know; the solution depends on that exchange's policy, which Pro Chart cannot change.

6.3) Copy trading on LBank only

Automatic copy trading is offered on LBank. Ourbit does not support it; an Ourbit user receives the signals and executes the trades themselves. This is the exchange's limitation, not the app's choice.

6.4) Continuity of access

Access continues for as long as the conditions above are met. If the balance falls below the required level, you are notified first and given time to top it up; after that period access is suspended until the balance is restored, and resumes automatically once it is. The thresholds and the grace period in force are announced in the app itself; the current policy is a minimum of 50 USDT to enter.
