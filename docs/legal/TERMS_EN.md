### 6.2) Membership conditions

To access signals and copy trading:

1. Register at **LBank** or **Ourbit** through **Pro Chart's dedicated link**. This step is not

2. Bring the account balance to at least **50 USDT**.
3. Enter your exchange account's **UID** in the app.
4. Verification runs automatically through the exchange's own API. Once confirmed, access is enabled.

If you already had an account opened without the dedicated link, let support know; the solution
depends on that exchange's policy, which Pro Chart cannot change.

Automatic copy trading is offered on **LBank**. **Ourbit** does not support it; an Ourbit user
receives the signals and executes the trades themselves. This is the exchange's limitation, not the
app's choice.
